# Code of Conduct

Azure Core Underground is dedicated to providing a harassment-free conference experience for everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity and expression, age, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, religion, or technology choices.

## Expected Behavior

- **Be respectful and inclusive** - Treat all participants with respect and consideration
- **Exercise consideration and empathy** - Be mindful of others' experiences and perspectives
- **Refrain from demeaning, discriminatory, or harassing behavior** - Keep discussions professional
- **Be mindful of your surroundings and fellow participants** - Alert organizers if you notice violations

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are considered harassment and are unacceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form
- Offensive verbal comments related to personal characteristics
- Inappropriate use of sexual images or language
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or following
- Sustained disruption of talks or events
- Unwelcome physical contact or attention

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the event organizers immediately. All reports will be handled with discretion.

**Contact:** [organizers@azurecoreunderground.com](mailto:organizers@azurecoreunderground.com)
